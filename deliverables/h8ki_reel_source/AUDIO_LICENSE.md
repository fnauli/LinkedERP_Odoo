# Audio rights – H8KI "The Human Algorithm" Reel

Every sound in this Reel (music and sound effects) is an **original work generated from
scratch by `audio.py`** using mathematical synthesis (Karplus-Strong plucked-string guitar,
sawtooth string pads, sine/square tones, filtered noise).

- No samples, loops, stems, presets or recordings from any third party are used.
- No existing melody or song is quoted or imitated; the chord progression
  (I–V/3–vi–IV) is a common musical building block and not protected by copyright.
- The audio can be regenerated at any time from the source in this folder.

The audio is therefore free to use commercially on Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube
Shorts, Facebook and paid ads, with no licence fee and no Content ID claim expected.
Upload it as "original audio".

If a library track is swapped in later: business/brand accounts on Meta platforms should
only use the **Meta Sound Collection** (not the general music library), or a track
licensed for commercial use (e.g. Artlist, Epidemic Sound, Musicbed).
